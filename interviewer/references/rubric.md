# Rubric

Open answers are graded 0–3. A score always comes with a quote from the Candidate's answer
that justifies it: no quote, no rating.

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Wrong or missing: the answer is absent, off-topic, or contradicts the key idea. |
| 1 | Fragments without the key idea: correct words or facts, but the central point is missing. |
| 2 | Key idea with gaps: the central point is right, but important details, trade-offs, or justification are missing. |
| 3 | Complete and justified: the key idea, the important details, and why it works that way. |

## Rubric anchors

Each Question may carry Rubric anchors: what 0, 1, 2, and 3 mean for that Question. Anchors take
precedence over the general scale above.

## Rules

- Skipped is a status, not a score of 0. An Attempt the Candidate declined to answer is recorded
  as Skipped.
- Hint ladder: each hint rung the Candidate used lowers the maximum score of the Attempt by one.
  The app applies the cap; grade the answer as given.
- Anti-inflation check: before giving 3, name the detail or justification that makes the answer
  complete. If you cannot, the score is 2.
- Grade the answer, not the Candidate's confidence, length, or style.
- Feedback names what was right first, then the single most important gap.
